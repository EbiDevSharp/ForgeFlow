# Slice 09 — Architectural & Design Decisions (`09-quality-traceability`)

| Decision ID | Topic | Approved Choice | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Q-Q01` | Stock Blocking Model | `BlockedBaseQuantity` on `InventoryBalance` (`AvailableBaseQuantity = OnHandBaseQuantity - BlockedBaseQuantity`) + `QualityInspection` | Separates physical warehouse location from quality status, supports partial lot release/scrap without artificial `TransferOut`/`TransferIn` ledger noise, and enforces `0 <= BlockedBaseQuantity <= OnHandBaseQuantity` under `UPDLOCK, HOLDLOCK`. |
| `Q-Q02` | Item Policy & Triggers | `Item.RequiresLotTracking` (`bool`) + `Item.QualityControlMode` (`None`/`IncomingReceipts`/`ProductionReceipts`/`AllReceipts`) + manual ad-hoc QC Holds | Supports strict lot traceability per item, automatic inbound QC gating where configured, and reactive QC Holds on any existing warehouse stock. |
| `Q-Q03` | Inspection Disposition | Partial disposition (`AcceptedBaseQuantity` release, `RejectedBaseQuantity` -> automatic `InventoryTransactionType.QualityScrap` adjustment, remainder kept in Quarantine) + optional `QualityInspectionLine` test measurements & `DefectReasonCode` | Matches real QC lab workflows while preserving append-only ledger accounting via explicit `QualityScrap` entries linked to `QualityInspectionId`. |
| `Q-Q04` | Lot Genealogy & Expiry | Recursive cycle-safe Forward & Backward genealogy from immutable `InventoryLedgerEntry` + `ProductionOrderId` (netting `Reversal`s) + `ItemLot` (`ManufactureDate`, `ExpirationDate`, `Notes`) | Avoids fragile duplicate genealogy tables while surfacing multi-level raw-to-WIP-to-FG lineage and lot dates/expiry status. |
| `Q-Q05` | RBAC & Navigation | `PermissionModule.Quality = 13` (`Quality.Read`, `Quality.Manage` as `OrganizationOrSite`, auto-granted to `OrganizationAdministrator`); `QualityNavGroup` -> `/quality` and `/traceability` + links from `/inventory` and `/production-orders` | Enforces separation of duties so warehouse clerks (`Inventory.Manage`) cannot release quarantined stock without `Quality.Manage`. |
| `Q-Q06` | Shortage & Expiry Enforcement | Slice 08 Material Shortage uses `AvailableBaseQuantity` (`OnHand - Blocked`) as usable supply (displaying `BlockedBaseQuantity` separately); expired lots (`ExpirationDate < Today`) are blocked from outbound `Issue`, `ProductionIssue`, and `TransferOut` unless QC updates expiry or scraps | Prevents production planners from counting quarantined stock as usable and prevents accidental consumption of expired materials. |
