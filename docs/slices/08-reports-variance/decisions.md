# Slice 08 — Architectural & Design Decisions (`08-reports-variance`)

| Decision ID | Topic | Choice | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Q-R01` | Persistence / Schema Impact | **Zero new tables or migrations** (`27` entities / `6` migrations unchanged) | All required operational facts (`ProductionOrder*`, `InventoryBalance`, `InventoryTransaction`, `InventoryLedgerEntry`, `UnitConversion`, `ItemUnitConversion`) already exist with site/org indexes (`Slice 05`–`07`). Keeps SQL queries fast via `AsNoTracking()` projections without schema bloat. |
| `Q-R02` | Permission Granularity | Two `OrganizationOrSite` permissions under `PermissionModule.Reports = 11`: `Reports.Read`, `Reports.Export` | Enables read-only analysts to view reports in `/reports` without granting inventory mutation or export privileges separately. Built-in `OrganizationAdministrator` receives both at org and site scopes. |
| `Q-R03` | Material Shortage Scope | Open orders in `Released` or `InProgress` status, non-sub-assembly (`!IsSubAssembly`) gross inputs normalized to `Item.BaseUnitId` vs physical `InventoryBalance.OnHandBaseQuantity` | Matches MRP net-requirements semantics: `Draft`, `Completed`, and `Cancelled` orders do not reserve active floor material, and sub-assembly intermediate lines are already exploded into leaf materials (`Slice 04`/`06`). |
| `Q-R04` | Variance Reversal Handling | Net `InventoryLedgerEntry.BaseQuantityDelta` by original transaction type (`ProductionIssue` + reversals targeting it; `ProductionReceipt` + reversals targeting it) | Reversals in `Slice 07` preserve `ProductionOrderId` and negate `BaseQuantityDelta`, and also reference `ReversedTransactionId`; grouping by the root transaction's `TransactionType` guarantees exact net issued and net received even after partial/full reversals. |
| `Q-R05` | CSV Download & Safety | Server-generated UTF-8 BOM (`\uFEFF`) CSV returned through `ReportingUseCases` (`Reports.Export`) with RFC-4180 quoting + formula-injection `'` prefix (`=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, `\t`, `\r`) | Guarantees localization, authorization enforcement (`Reports.Export`), Persian Excel compatibility, and protection against CSV formula injection on untrusted user notes/codes. |
