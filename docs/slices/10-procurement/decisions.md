# Slice 10 — Architectural & Design Decisions (`10-procurement`)

| Decision ID | Topic | Approved Choice | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| `P-Q01` | Supplier Ownership | Organization-shared `Supplier` (`Code`, `Name`, optional contact fields, `IsActive`) | Mirrors `Unit`, `Item`, and `Bom` ownership; avoids duplicate vendor masters across sites within one organization while reserving inactive codes. |
| `P-Q02` | PO State Machine & Short-Close | `Draft (1)` → `Approved (2)` → `PartiallyReceived (3)` → `Received (4)`; `Cancelled (5)` from `Draft`/`Approved`; `Closed (6)` from `PartiallyReceived` | Freezes ordered quantities & base unit snapshots at approval, supports partial deliveries, allows canceling unreceived orders, and explicitly closes short deliveries (`Closed`). |
| `P-Q03` | No Over-Receipt & Unit Flexibility | Strict `ReceivedBaseQuantity + incomingBase <= OrderedBaseQuantity`; lines & receipts convert through `UnitConversionGraph` to `BaseUnitIdSnapshot` | Prevents duplicate/erroneous over-receipts under concurrent submissions while allowing ordering in pack/ton units and receiving in kg/pcs. |
| `P-Q04` | Atomic Inventory, Lot, QC & Reversal | Single `Serializable` transaction linking `InventoryTransaction.PurchaseOrderId` → `PurchaseOrder`; respects `RequiresLotTracking` & `QualityControlMode.IncomingReceipts`; `ReverseTransaction` restores `ReceivedBaseQuantity` & PO status | Reuses Slice 07/09 accounting and quarantine workflows with zero reconciliation drift on reversal or QC rejection. |
| `P-Q05` | Currency & Optional Pricing | Optional `UnitPrice >= 0` (`decimal(18,4)`) per line + 3-letter `CurrencyCode` (default `IRR`, supported `IRR`/`USD`/`EUR`) | Supports both pure quantity replenishment teams and cost-tracked procurement without forcing prices when unknown at draft time. |
| `P-Q06` | RBAC, Shortage & Navigation | `PermissionModule.Procurement = 14` (`Procurement.Read`, `Procurement.ManageSuppliers`, `Procurement.ManageOrders`, `Procurement.ReceiveOrders`); informational `OnOrderBaseQuantity` on `/reports`; `ProcurementNavGroup` between `MasterData` and `Inventory` | Enforces buyer/receiver separation of duties, surfaces incoming replenishment supply in Shortage reports without masking physical shortfalls, and follows operational workflow order. |
